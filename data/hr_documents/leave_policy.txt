ABC CORPORATION — LEAVE POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Annual Leave:
All full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave
per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata
basis. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3
consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Maternity Leave:
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
This can be split as 8 weeks before and 18 weeks after delivery.
Employees must notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected
delivery date.

Paternity Leave:
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave
within 30 days of the child's birth. This must be taken in one
continuous block.

Emergency Leave:
Employees are entitled to 3 days of emergency leave per year for
immediate family bereavement or medical emergencies.

Notice Period:
The notice period for resignation is 30 days for employees with
less than 2 years of service, and 60 days for employees with
2 or more years of service.

Leave Application Process:
Employees must apply for leave at least 2 weeks in advance through
the HR portal at hr.acmecorp.com. Emergency leave requests will be
considered on a case by case basis. Leave applications require
direct manager approval.

Leave without Pay:
Employees may apply for leave without pay after exhausting all
paid leave entitlements. Maximum leave without pay is 30 days
per year and requires HR Director approval.
