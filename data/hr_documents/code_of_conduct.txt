ABC CORPORATION — CODE OF CONDUCT
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Workplace Behavior:
All employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will result
in immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Confidentiality:
Employees must not share company confidential information with
external parties. This includes client data, financial information,
product roadmaps, and internal processes. Violation may result in
legal action.

Data Privacy:
Employee personal data is stored securely and used only for
employment purposes. Employees have the right to access their
personal data upon written request to HR.

Disciplinary Process:
First violation: Verbal warning documented in employee file.
Second violation: Written warning with performance improvement plan.
Third violation: Termination of employment.
Gross misconduct may result in immediate termination.

Working Hours:
Standard working hours are 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday.
A one hour lunch break is provided. Flexible working arrangements
must be approved by direct manager and HR in writing.

IT and Device Policy:
Company devices must be used for business purposes only.
Personal use of company devices is prohibited.
Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.

Conflict of Interest:
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their
manager and HR. Working for a competitor while employed at ACME
is strictly prohibited.

Social Media:
Employees must not post confidential company information on social
media. Employees are responsible for content they post publicly
that could impact the company's reputation.
